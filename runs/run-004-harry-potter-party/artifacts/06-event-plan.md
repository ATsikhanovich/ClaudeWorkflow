## Summary

A Harry Potter themed reunion dinner for **10 friends** on **Friday 23 October 2026, in the
evening**, at **The Alchemist Grill & Cocktail, ul. Grzybowska 5a, Warsaw** — in its private
**Sala VIP**. The venue was chosen precisely because its own character carries the theme: it
brands itself around alchemy, has a dimly lit interior fitted with vintage alchemical
equipment, and serves signature drinks named "Philosopher's Stone", "Elixir of Life" and
"Alchemist's Secret". No decorations, props, entertainment or hired staff are booked, so the
wizarding atmosphere comes entirely from the room itself and from the food and drink.

The evening is a **seated, family-style three-stage themed dinner** — a shared starter board,
a lava-grill main course and a shared dessert — opening with a themed welcome "potion" serve
for each guest, together with soft drinks for the table. Catering is delivered by the venue's
own kitchen and bar, so there is **one restaurant bill, not a hire fee plus an external
caterer**. The plan costs **1,850–2,200 PLN** (planning figure 2,000 PLN), or **1,943–2,420 PLN**
including the planning reserve, against the confirmed **4,000 PLN** all-in limit — leaving
roughly **1,580–2,057 PLN of headroom**. Drinks beyond the welcome serve and soft drinks are
paid individually by guests.

**The most important caveat:** every figure in this plan is an **estimate, not a quote**. The
venue publishes no prices at all — no hire fee, no minimum spend, no menu prices — and
answers each enquiry with an individually prepared offer. Nothing here is booked, and nothing
here is priced by the venue. The booking actions in this plan must be completed in writing
before anything is confirmed.
