## Summary

Because the venue chosen for its wizarding character has its own kitchen and bars no outside caterer, catering for this event is the **food-and-drink offering of The Alchemist's own kitchen and cocktail bar**, designed here as a themed set menu for 10 plus a "potion" welcome serve and soft drinks. The venue publishes **no prices at all** and its food menu only as three image files, so every component is **price on request**; the estimate is built on published Warsaw comparators (U Fukiera à la carte and by-the-glass prices; a published Warsaw group-catering rate of "od 136 zł netto / os.") and on published Warsaw café-bar cocktail and soft-drink prices. The recommended option is a **three-stage sharing menu** (starter board, lava-grill mains, dessert) served **family-style** in the Sala VIP, with each guest receiving a "potion" welcome drink and the table receiving soft drinks: **≈185–220 PLN per person, 1,850–2,200 PLN total, planning figure 2,000 PLN**. The Harry Potter register is delivered by course naming, by the venue's own published alchemical signature drinks ("Elixir of Life", "Philosopher's Stone", "Alchemist's Secret") and by its lava-stone table theatre — the venue does **not** publish any butterbeer-style drink, so that is framed as a request, not as a menu item. Critically, this catering total **is** the restaurant bill and therefore already clears any compulsory minimum spend; the minimum spend must **not** also be added as a separate venue cost.

## Details

### 1. Scope decision (structural, already made — not re-opened here)

- The venue's own pages present only its in-house culinary and bar offering and publish no permission for an outside caterer (S1–S4); the same is true of every venue on the shortlist (see 02-venue.md). The user therefore chose, on 2026-10-15, that **the chosen venue's kitchen and bar deliver the catering**.
- Consequence for this artifact: there is no external caterer to select. The deliverable is a **themed menu concept and cost for the venue's own kitchen**: the set-menu structure, the dishes (drawn only from what the venue publishes), the welcome-drink plan, the service style and the per-person cost.
- If the venue were ever to allow an outside caterer (it does not publish that it does), this artifact would have to be re-run; nothing here pre-supposes it.

### 2. Cost model — the double-counting rule (explicit, for the budget-aggregator)

1. The **venue line** carries **only the hire fee**. No hire fee is published; the planning assumption is **0 PLN**, to be confirmed in writing (consistent with 02-venue.md).
2. The **catering line in this artifact carries the actual food-and-drink bill** — the single restaurant invoice for the evening.
3. Any **compulsory minimum spend is a floor on the catering line, not an additive venue cost.** The 1,200–1,500 PLN figure in 02-venue.md is the estimated floor of that same bill.
4. **Do not enter the venue minimum spend (1,200–1,500 PLN) and this food bill as two separate additive costs — that double counts the same restaurant bill.** The recommended catering line (1,850–2,200 PLN) already exceeds the estimated floor, so once the venue confirms its minimum spend, **nothing is added to the venue line for it**.
5. If the venue's confirmed minimum spend turns out to be **higher** than the food and drink actually ordered, the shortfall is charged by the venue and belongs to the **catering line** (the bill must be lifted to the minimum spend); the venue line still carries only the hire fee.
6. Practical effect on gate G1: a catering line of 1,850–2,200 PLN (planning figure 2,000 PLN) plus an assumed 0 PLN hire fee stays inside the 4,000 PLN all-in ceiling, with the minimum-spend floor absorbed inside the catering line rather than stacked on top of it. The full budget total is the budget-aggregator's artifact, not this one.

### 7. Service style

**Recommended: seated, family-style (shared boards and the lava grill in the middle of the table) in the Sala VIP.** Reasons: the venue's own service model is dishes "served on hot basalt lava" plus "przekąski, dania główne, desery" (S1, S3), which is a sharing format by nature; staffing as a booked service is explicitly **not** required by the requirements, and family-style keeps service inside the venue's ordinary restaurant operation; and a single table of 10 in a 40-seat private room supports it.

Alternatives, both real options at this venue: **plated set menu** (the venue states it serves mains, snacks and desserts as separate courses — S3), and a **standing cocktail reception** (Sala VIP is published at 40–50 standing — S3), which would suit a shorter, drinks-led evening but would not deliver the themed food concept as well.
