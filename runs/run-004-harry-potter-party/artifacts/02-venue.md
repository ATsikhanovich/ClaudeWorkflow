## Summary

Four real Warsaw indoor venues were researched and compared against the confirmed requirements:
The Alchemist Grill & Cocktail (ul. Grzybowska 5a), U Fukiera (Rynek Starego Miasta 27), the Museum of
Warsaw's restored Old Town cellars at ul. Brzozowa together with the Pharmacy Museum, and Południk Zero
(ul. Wilcza 25). **The Alchemist (Grzybowska 5a) is recommended**: it is the only shortlisted venue whose
own published character is explicitly alchemical/wizard-like (alchemy branding, vintage alchemical decor,
"Philosopher's Stone" / "Elixir of Life" cocktails), and it has a genuinely separate private room — Sala VIP,
40 seated — which comfortably satisfies capacity ≥10 for a seated dinner. No venue on the shortlist publishes
a private-room price, so **all venue costs are "price on request"**; the planning figure for the venue line is
**1,200–1,500 PLN (hire fee assumed 0 PLN + estimated compulsory minimum spend), to be confirmed in writing**.
One structural risk must be carried forward: every shortlisted venue is a restaurant/café with its own kitchen
and none publishes permission to bring an outside caterer, so the plan should expect a single food-and-drink
bill rather than a separate hire fee plus a separate external caterer.

### Recommended venue

**The Alchemist Grill & Cocktail — ul. Grzybowska 5a, 00-132 Warszawa** (Sala VIP, private)

Justification against the confirmed requirements:

- **Capacity ≥ guest count (gate G2).** The private Sala VIP seats 40 (40–50 standing against a party of 10);
  the main hall seats 80. 40 ≥ 10, so the requirement is met with a wide margin, in a room the venue itself
  presents as the private-room option for "a private or corporate occasion party".
- **Theme (the binding constraint).** The requirements forbid booked decorations, so the atmosphere has to come
  from the venue. This is the only shortlisted venue whose own character is published as explicitly
  alchemical/magical: the venue brands itself around "the dark arts of molecular mixology" and calls itself a
  restaurant and cocktail bar "where every bite and every sip is real magic for your senses"; an independent
  guide describes a dimly lit interior decorated with vintage alchemical equipment with an "enchanting vibe"
  and signature drinks named **"Philosopher's Stone"**, **"Elixir of Life"** and **"Alchemist's Secret"**; a
  Polish review of the same brand describes "magicians behind the bar" and drinks that smoke, bubble, hiss and
  change colour, in separate partitioned spaces. For a Harry Potter themed reunion with no props, this is the
  closest verified match among real Warsaw venues.
- **Budget.** No hire fee, cennik or minimum spend is published anywhere on the venue's site — enquiries are
  answered with an individually prepared offer. Planning figure for the venue line: **1,200–1,500 PLN**
  (hire fee assumed **0 PLN** + estimated compulsory minimum spend **1,200–1,500 PLN**). Estimate basis:
  (a) no price is published by this venue; (b) a comparable Warsaw old-town restaurant with published à la
  carte prices (U Fukiera: starters 43–92 PLN, mains 53–210 PLN, desserts 39–59 PLN) implies roughly
  120–160 PLN per person for a two-course dinner, i.e. 1,200–1,600 PLN for 10; (c) the confirmed 4,000 PLN
  all-in ceiling. This is an estimate, not a quote.
- **Location.** Central Warsaw (Grzybowska 5a); the venue states both of its locations are central and
  transit-accessible, including nearby metro stations, and it also runs a second branch at ul. Poznańska 16.
- **Venue type / date.** Indoor (requirement met); it is a restaurant with an evening service — availability
  for Friday 2026-10-23 must be confirmed (see open issues).
- **Accessibility.** Not published for this venue — to confirm. This is **not** a hard requirement here: the
  user confirmed "no access needs" (no step-free access, accessible WC or mobility-aid seating required), so
  this is recorded for information only and does not disqualify the venue.

Named fallback: **U Fukiera** (Rynek Starego Miasta 27) — even stronger historic-cellar credibility (the oldest
restaurant in Warsaw, with a wine store opened in its cellars at the beginning of the 16th century; the
Winiarnia hall is a former wine cellar), published opening hours to 23:30 on Fridays, and a listing that
describes it as friendly for people with disabilities. It is second only because no private-room capacity or
price is published and its kitchen is strictly in-house.

### Structural cost-model note for the budget-aggregator (important)

All four shortlisted venues are restaurants/cafés with their own kitchens, and none publishes permission for an
outside caterer. The clean "venue hire fee + separately booked external caterer" split is therefore unlikely to
be available at a themed Warsaw venue inside this budget: expect **one food-and-drink bill**. Recommended
treatment: enter the venue line as **1,200–1,500 PLN** (the compulsory minimum spend, i.e. the floor of the
bill) and record the food and drink actually ordered above that floor under catering; do **not** add a separate
hire fee and a separate external caterer on top of the same bill — that would double count. If the coordinator
instead wants a strictly separated hire-fee + outside-caterer structure, it would require a plain rented room
with no wizarding character, which would fail the theme requirement — that trade-off must be surfaced to the
user, not silently applied.
