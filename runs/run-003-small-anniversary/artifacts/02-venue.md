## Summary

**Recommended: U Fukiera (Warsaw Old Town)** — Warsaw's oldest restaurant (tradition back
to the early 16th century, repeatedly listed by the Michelin Guide) with a dedicated
**private-dining room** where the family dines alone, exactly the format requested: the
restaurant explicitly offers private dinners for occasions such as anniversaries and
customises menu and arrangement on request. The menu publishes clear prices, so dinner
costs can be planned from a real card (see the catering artifact).

**Why U Fukiera wins:** it is the requested Old Town atmosphere, the private room is the
core of their offer (including anniversaries), prices are transparent from the published
card, and the kitchen accommodates menu preferences directly ("describe your culinary and
arrangement preferences").

**Accessibility (as required in revision 2):** the venue is a historic Old Town building;
the organiser must confirm step-free access to the private room and a wide-aisle seating
layout for one guest using a walking aid before the date (recorded as an arrangement to
make in the logistics artifact; the restaurant handles arrangement preferences on
request).
