## Summary

A warm 25th wedding-anniversary dinner for 12 family members on Saturday 5 December 2026
in the private dining room of **U Fukiera** in Warsaw's Old Town — three courses from the
restaurant's published menu (with a full vegetarian path for two guests), a prosecco
welcome toast, wine with dinner and a family speech after dessert. Guests now arrive
**from 18:30** so the first course is served at 19:00 sharp, and the seating plan
includes a wide aisle and staff assistance for one guest using a walking aid. The plan
costs **4 631.55 PLN** against the 5 000 PLN limit. There is deliberately no
entertainment — conversation and family toasts are the program.

## Venue

**U Fukiera, Warsaw Old Town** — Warsaw's oldest restaurant (tradition back to the early
16th century, repeatedly listed by the Michelin Guide) with a dedicated private dining
room where the family dines alone. Menu and arrangement are customised on request, the
card publishes clear prices, and the restaurant explicitly hosts anniversaries. Fallback:
Arche Hotel Krakowska (private rooms, wege/gluten-free menu variants). The private-room
fee is quoted on request (500 PLN reserved).

## Weather outlook and contingency

Early December in Warsaw: the outlook for 5 December 2026 shows **+10.9 °C / +3.1 °C**
with 4.4 mm precipitation and gusts up to 19.6 km/h (light drizzle); the same date in
2024–2025 ranged +4.6…+8.4 °C. **Contingency: not applicable — indoor restaurant
dinner.** Practical effect: guests are advised to travel door-to-door and the host
arranges covered drop-off — which also serves the accessibility plan.

## Catering

Three courses from the published menu (**3 511.00 PLN** food and drinks together,
≈293 PLN/guest): roasted-tomato cream with ginger (49 PLN), a main of trout with
pistachio skin (114 PLN) or vegetarian risotto (89 PLN — five servings, covering the two
vegetarian guests), plum dumplings for dessert (54 PLN), a prosecco welcome toast (40 PLN
per glass) and one glass of wine with dinner (46 PLN). Strictly vegetarian preparation is
confirmed at booking.

## Entertainment

**Not applicable — no entertainment required.** The family's program is conversation and
toasts; nothing is booked and nothing is budgeted.

## Logistics

Flowers and centrepieces (400 PLN: florist arrangement + four rented candle bowls) are
delivered at 17:00; the restaurant provides the table setting and service. **Accessibility
arrangements** (added after the family's review): covered drop-off near the entrance,
confirmation of a step-free route into the private room (historic building — staff
assistance arranged if a step remains), wide-aisle seating beside the guest's chair and
cloakroom assistance at the room entrance.
