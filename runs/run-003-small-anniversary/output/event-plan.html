Venue

U Fukiera, Warsaw Old Town — the city's oldest restaurant (tradition back to the early 16th century, repeatedly listed by the Michelin Guide), offering a dedicated private dining room where the family dines alone. Menu and arrangements are customised on request, and the restaurant explicitly hosts anniversaries and family occasions.

  • Private room reserved exclusively for the 12 guests
  • Classic Old-Polish cuisine from a published, transparent menu
  • Fallback if unavailable: a Warsaw hotel restaurant with private rooms and wege/gluten-free menu variants

Weather outlook & contingency

Early December in Warsaw: the outlook for 5 December 2026 shows +10.9 °C / +3.1 °C with about 4.4 mm precipitation (light drizzle) and gusts up to 19.6 km/h; the same date in past years ranged +4.6…+8.4 °C.

The dinner is fully indoors. Practical effect: guests travel door-to-door and the host arranges a covered drop-off near the entrance — which also serves the accessibility plan below.

Contingency: not applicable — indoor restaurant dinner.

Catering

Three courses from the restaurant's published menu, with a full vegetarian path for two guests. Food and drinks together: 3 511.00 PLN (~293 PLN per guest).

  • Starter — roasted-tomato cream with ginger (49 PLN)
  • Main — trout with crispy pistachio skin, roasted potatoes and rucola (114 PLN) · or vegetarian risotto (89 PLN)
  • Dessert — plum dumplings with plum-butter sauce (54 PLN)
  • Drinks — prosecco welcome toast (40 PLN per glass) and one glass of wine with dinner (46 PLN)

Strictly vegetarian preparation is confirmed with the kitchen at booking.

Entertainment

Not applicable — no entertainment is planned. The family's program is conversation, toasts and the anniversary speech after dessert; nothing is booked and nothing is budgeted for acts or music.

Logistics

  • Flowers & table decor — a seasonal flower arrangement for the anniversary table plus candle centrepieces (400 PLN), delivered at 17:00; the restaurant provides the table setting and service.
  • Accessibility arrangements — covered drop-off near the entrance; a step-free route into the private room is confirmed in advance (staff assistance arranged if a step remains); the table is set with a wide aisle beside the guest's chair; staff take coats at the room entrance.
  • Transport — guests arrive on their own or by taxi; taxis are arranged for departure at 23:00.
