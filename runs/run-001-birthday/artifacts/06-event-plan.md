## Summary

A 40-guest indoor 30th birthday party on Saturday 14 November 2026, 16:00–24:00, at
**LOFT HOUSE CLUB** in Warsaw Praga. The all-in plan costs **5 874.20 PLN** against the
6 000 PLN limit (125.80 PLN headroom) and covers everything the host confirmed: private

## Venue

**LOFT HOUSE CLUB, Warsaw Praga** — private party venue for up to 50 guests, booked as
the "Pakiet Małego Imprezowicza": 8 hours of party (16:00–24:00) plus 2 hours of
preparation (14:00–16:00), professional sound and lighting, security with entry
wristbands, event-organisation support, fridge/freezer for guest drinks and **no
corkage**. Published price: **3 500 PLN**.

Fallback if the date is taken: a central-Warsaw bar event space advertising 40–50-guest
private parties with catering and AV equipment (Nowogrodzka street).

## Weather outlook and contingency

Mid-November in Warsaw: the seasonal outlook for 14 November 2026 shows **+8.6 °C / +1.3
°C**, **6.5 mm** precipitation and gusts up to **26.1 km/h** (drizzle); the same date in
2024–2025 ranged +5.5…+10.9 °C. Expect a cool, damp evening. **Contingency: not
applicable — indoor event**; the practical measure is advising guests to plan door-to-door
transport and confirming the cloakroom at booking.

## Catering

Light party food at published Warsaw menu prices (**1 136.20 PLN**, ≈ 28.4 PLN/guest):
24 large pizzas via "3 × large pizza" deals (with Margherita, Vege Mania and Grecka/
Serowa making roughly 60 % of the food vegetarian), Italian salads, coleslaw, warm
stuffed mushrooms, homemade lemonade and canned soft drinks. Pizza order is placed the
day before with delivery at 16:45; vegetarian pizzas are labelled at the buffet.
Alcohol is brought by guests (no corkage).

## Entertainment

**Live acoustic duo — 2 × 40 minutes** (set 1 at 18:30, set 2 at 20:00) on the venue's
house PA and lighting. Reservation: **1 000 PLN**, inside the published 900–1 500 PLN
market range for this format; final quote pending from a Warsaw provider (duo, vocals +
guitar/piano). If the quote exceeds 1 125 PLN, the fallback is a single piano/vocal set.

## Logistics

Decorations are rented (napkins, charger plates, decorative bowls, stands — **238 PLN**,
delivery/pick-up in Warsaw). Sound, lighting, security, entry wristbands and drinks
cooling are provided by the venue package. Guests arrive on their own (public
transport/taxi). No permits needed — everything runs inside a licensed private venue.
