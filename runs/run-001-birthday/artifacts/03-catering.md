## Summary

Order-in party food rather than a caterer: **24 large pizzas** (8 × "3 large pizza" deals),
warm sides and soft drinks, bought at published Warsaw menu prices for **1 136.20 PLN**
total (~28 PLN/guest). Vegetarian guests are covered by dedicated vegetarian pizzas and
the side dishes. This is the only catering style that fits 6 000 PLN all-in — full
catering in Warsaw starts around 100 PLN/guest and wedding-grade buffet catering runs
380–480 PLN/guest.

### Dietary coverage

- **Vegetarian:** at least 3 of every 5 pizzas are vegetarian (Margherita, Vege Mania,
  Grecka/Serowa), plus both salads and the stuffed mushrooms — roughly 60 % of the food
  volume is meat-free.
- Alcohol: guests bring their own; the venue charges no corkage and provides a fridge and
  freezer.

### Why not a professional caterer (cost check)

Market references fetched for this run: vegetarian grill catering averages **136 PLN per
person** (90–165 PLN range) and wedding-grade buffet catering in Warsaw runs **380–480
PLN per person** before drinks. Even the cheapest professional package (40 × 90 =
3 600 PLN) would leave under 2 500 PLN for venue + live music. The order-in party-food
format keeps the confirmed budget intact.
