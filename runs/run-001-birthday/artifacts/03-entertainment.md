## Summary

Book a **Warsaw acoustic duo for 2 × 40 minutes** (arrival ~18:15, sets at 18:30 and
20:00). Market pricing for exactly this format is published at **900–1 500 PLN** per
performance; 1 000 PLN is reserved in the budget (lower-middle of the range) and the
provider quotes on request. Since the venue package already includes sound and lighting,
the musicians only need the house PA — which keeps the quote at the low end.

## Details

### Format

| Option | Format | Suits | Price |

| **Recommended: acoustic duo, 2 × 40 min** | vocals + guitar/piano | 30–50-guest private party | 900–1 500 PLN per performance (market range) |
| Full cover band, 3 × 45 min | 3–5 musicians | 80–120-guest corporate events | 2 500–4 500 PLN per performance (market range) |
| DJ (venue package "Vixiarza") | DJ hosting + 10 XXL pizzas | dance-focused party | bundled at 5 200 PLN venue package |

The full band (2 500 PLN+) and the DJ package (−1 700 PLN vs. the chosen venue package)
were both rejected: the band would blow the budget, and the user explicitly asked for
live music rather than a DJ.

### Candidate providers (Warsaw)

- **Band4Event (Warsaw / Mazowieckie)** — 15+ years on the market, offers exactly this
  kind of small line-up: duos (vocals + guitar/piano), jazz/soul and pop sets, own sound
  and lighting, plays private parties and occasions. Quote on request (their contact form
  takes date + venue + guest profile).
- The venue's own sound/lighting is included in the package, so the duo performs on the
  house PA — worth stating in the quote request (market data shows this lowers band
  prices by roughly 600–1 200 PLN for corporate line-ups).

### Technical needs

House PA and lighting (included in the venue package), 2 vocal mics + DI for guitar —
covered. Power access from 18:00; soundcheck not needed before guest arrival (acoustic
format), per market guidance a separate afternoon soundcheck would add 300–800 PLN.
