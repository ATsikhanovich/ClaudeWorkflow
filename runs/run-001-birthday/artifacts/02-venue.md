## Summary

**Recommended: LOFT HOUSE CLUB (Warszawa Praga)** — a private party venue for up to 50
guests whose published "Pakiet Małego Imprezowicza" covers 8 hours of party (+2 h
preparation), security, professional sound and lighting, event support, **no corkage**
and BYO food/drinks, for **3 500 PLN**. It fits the guest count, is indoor, removes any
external sound-equipment cost, and leaves ~2 500 PLN for food, music and decorations.

**Why the recommended option wins:** capacity 50 ≥ 40 guests (gate G2); indoor as
required; the package price already includes security, sound and lighting, which removes
separate equipment rental from the budget; guests bring their own drinks with no corkage,
exactly matching the confirmed preference. The saving versus the DJ package (−1 700 PLN)
is what funds the required live music.

**Alternatives considered:** a bar-style event space in central Warsaw advertising
40–50 guest capacity with catering and AV equipment (Jabeerwocky, Nowogrodzka street) —
attractive, but it prices on request and pushes food/drink through the venue, which does
not fit the BYO preference; kept as the fallback if LOFT HOUSE CLUB is unavailable on
2026-11-14.

**Accessibility:** the venue page does not publish accessibility details; step-free
access is listed as an open issue to confirm by phone before booking (no accessibility
requirements were declared, so this is informational).
