## Summary

Almost everything is provided by the venue package; only **decorations are rented
externally (238 PLN, published rental prices)**. Equipment, staffing and permits are
covered or not applicable; guests arrive on their own. Nothing is double-booked with the
venue.

### Transport & parking

Not applicable — guests make their own way (public transport/taxi; the mid-November
weather outlook supports door-to-door transport). No coach or shuttle required.

### Staffing

Venue security and organisation support (package). Two friends handle the birthday cake
and the guest book; no paid staffing required.

### Permits & compliance

Not applicable — the event runs entirely inside a licensed private venue (no public
space, no amplified outdoor sound). House rules on noise end time apply (confirm cutoff
at booking).
