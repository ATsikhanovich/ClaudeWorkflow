Venue

LOFT HOUSE CLUB (Warsaw Praga) — a private party venue for up to 50 guests, booked as the eight-hour party package with two hours of preparation from 14:00. The package price of 3 500 PLN covers the space, professional sound and lighting, security with entry wristbands, event-organisation support, a fridge and freezer for guest drinks and no corkage.

The space is fully indoor — the right call for a mid-November evening. Fallback if the date is taken: a central-Warsaw bar event space offering private parties for 40–50 guests with catering and AV equipment.

  • Capacity 50 ≥ 40 guests
  • Guests bring their own drinks — no corkage, coolers provided
  • Sound and lighting included (also used by the musicians)

Weather outlook & contingency

Mid-November in Warsaw is cool and damp: the outlook for 14 November 2026 shows +8.6 °C / +1.3 °C with about 6.5 mm precipitation and gusts up to 26.1 km/h (drizzle). The same date in past years ranged +5.5…+10.9 °C.

The party is fully indoors, so there is no weather-dependent part of the program. Practical note for guests: plan door-to-door transport (taxi or public transport) rather than walking between venues on a wet November evening.

Contingency: not applicable — indoor event.

Catering

Light party food served buffet-style from 17:00 (pizzas delivered warm at 16:45), plus soft drinks. Alcohol is brought by guests. Total food & drinks: 1 136.20 PLN (~28 PLN per guest).

  • 24 large pizzas — including Margherita, Vege Mania and Grecka/Serowa for the vegetarian guests
  • Italian salad and coleslaw
  • Warm stuffed mushrooms in garlic sauce
  • Homemade lemonade and canned soft drinks

Vegetarian pizzas are labelled at the buffet; roughly 60% of the food volume is meat-free.

Entertainment

Live acoustic duo — 2 × 40 minutes on the venue's house sound system: set 1 at 18:30, set 2 at 20:00. Budget reservation 1 000 PLN (market range for this format: 900–1 500 PLN; final quote pending from a Warsaw provider).

The late evening is open-floor dancing with the house sound and lighting, and the birthday cake moment lands at 20:40 between the second set and the dance block.

Logistics

  • Decorations — rented napkins, charger plates, decorative bowls and stands (238 PLN), delivered and set by 16:00.
  • Sound & lighting — included in the venue package.
  • Drinks — guests bring their own; no corkage; fridge and freezer on site.
  • Transport — guests arrive on their own; door-to-door advised (see weather).
  • Staffing — venue security and support included; two friends handle the cake and the guest book.
