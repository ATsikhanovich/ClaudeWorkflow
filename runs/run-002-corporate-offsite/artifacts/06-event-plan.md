## Summary

A full-day corporate offsite for 80 people on Friday 23 April 2027 at **Villa Foksal** in
central Warsaw — garden plus orangery and rooms, so the outdoor parts of the day always
have an indoor twin. Morning workshop indoors with rented AV, lean catering (welcome
coffee + hot working lunch with vegetarian and gluten-free options), an organised Warsaw
city game in the afternoon and two coaches from/to the HQ. The plan costs **19 808.25 PLN
against the hard 20 000 PLN ceiling** (headroom 191.75 PLN) after one budget revision:
the original all-day catering package was trimmed to the confirmed lean service level.

## Venue

**Villa Foksal, Foksal 3/5** — a historic villa with combinable restaurant rooms, an
orangery and a garden, hosting up to 400 people. Its own kitchen handles food and diet
arrangements, and for outdoor events the venue always agrees a parallel under-roof
variant — which is exactly the mandatory fallback for this day. Fallback option if the
date is taken: Arche Hotel Krakowska (garden, restaurant, hotel rooms, wege/gluten-free
menu variants). Venue hire is a budget allocation of 4 500 PLN pending the venue's
preliminary quote.

## Weather outlook and contingency

Late-April Warsaw: the seasonal outlook for 23 April 2027 shows **+11.9 °C / +4.5 °C**
(precipitation/wind not yet published at this lead time); the same date in 2025–2026 was
dry and overcast at **+16.8…+21.4 °C** with wind up to 24 km/h. **Contingency:** the
morning workshop is always indoors; the city game runs outdoors unless it rains or the
feels-like temperature is below +8 °C, in which case the organiser's indoor variant runs
instead — same timetable, no re-scheduling.

## Catering

Lean service level, served by the venue's in-house kitchen (**8 240 PLN**, ≈103 PLN/guest):
welcome coffee break at 08:45 (coffee, tea, water, pastries) and a hot working-lunch
buffet at 12:30 with two hot dishes (one vegetarian as a main), grains, salads, bread,
fruit and drinks. Vegetarian and gluten-free options are labelled and arranged with the
kitchen directly.

## Entertainment

An **organised Warsaw city game** in the afternoon (13:45–16:15): 80 participants split
into 6–10-person teams solving puzzles with a dedicated coordinator. The organiser
(active since 2006, 3 000+ corporate events) includes participant insurance and a
weather Plan B. Allocation: **3 290 PLN** (their lowest published per-attraction price
point; final quote pending). No live music, per the confirmed requirements.

## Logistics

AV (projector, screen, sound, two microphones, cable ramps) rented at published day rates
— **1 015 PLN**. Transport: two coaches, HQ → venue 07:45–08:45 and venue → HQ
16:45–17:30, **1 820 PLN** at the published Warsaw hourly rate of 260 PLN/h. Staffing is
covered by the venue service team and the activity coordinator. No permits required on
the company side.
