## Summary

AV is rented at **published day rates (1 015 PLN)**, transport is **2 coaches × 3.5 h at
the published Warsaw hourly rate (1 820 PLN)**, staffing is covered by the venue and the
activity organiser, and no permits are needed. Nothing overlaps with what the venue or
the organiser already provides.

### Transport

Two coaches, HQ (Warsaw) → venue 07:45–08:45 and venue → HQ 16:45–17:30: about 3.5
charged hours per coach at the **published Warsaw hourly rate of 260 PLN/h** → 1 820 PLN.
(Alternative considered: individual arrival — rejected, the requirement fixes coach
transport.)

### Staffing

Venue service team (rooms, catering) and the activity organiser's coordinator cover the
day. One internal event lead from the company attends as the single point of contact —
no paid extra staffing.

### Permits & compliance

Not applicable — the workshop and meals run at a private venue; the city game is executed
by a licensed organiser that carries participant insurance and coordinates any public-space
permissions itself. No public-space permits are pulled by the company.
