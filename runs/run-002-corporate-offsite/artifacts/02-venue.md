## Summary

**Recommended: Villa Foksal (Foksal 3/5, central Warsaw)** — a historic villa with
several combinable restaurant rooms, an orangery and a **garden** for outdoor parts of the
day. It hosts up to 400 people, its own kitchen handles catering and diet arrangements,
and — critically for gate G6 — for outdoor events the venue **always agrees a parallel
indoor backup**. Pricing is quote-based ("preliminary quote within 24 business hours"),
so 4 500 PLN is a budget allocation for the room-and-service component, not a quoted
price.

## Details

| Arche Hotel Krakowska | Sala Mars / Perła / Fuksja + Restauracja Impresja + garden | 250+ | garden and lobby | air-conditioned rooms, hotel infrastructure | in-house, wege and gluten-free menu variants | individual quote |

**Why Villa Foksal wins:** the garden covers the "outdoor-capable" requirement, the
orangery/rooms cover the mandatory fallback (venue states they always plan the under-roof
variant in parallel for outdoor events), the in-house kitchen settles menus and diets in
one place — and the venue has explicit corporate-event references (gala, integration
meetings, conferences).

**Accessibility:** the venue is a historic villa in central Warsaw; step-free routes
should be confirmed at booking (no accessibility requirement was declared).

**Transport note:** central address means guests could arrive individually, but the
confirmed requirement fixes 2-coach transport from the HQ — kept in logistics.
