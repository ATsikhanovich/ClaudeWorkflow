## Summary

Book an **organised Warsaw city game** (team-based urban game with puzzles and tasks) for
80 participants, run by a Warsaw team-building agency operating since 2006 with 3 000+
corporate events, 89 catalogue activities, dedicated coordinators and — importantly —
**transport, participant insurance and a coordinator included in their full-day packages,
plus a documented weather Plan B** (move activities under cover). Budget allocation:
**3 290 PLN**, the lowest per-attraction price point published in the organiser's Warsaw
catalogue.

## Details

| Option | Format | Group fit | Weather Plan B | Price |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------------|-------|
| **Warsaw city game (recommended)** | teams solve puzzles across the city/venue grounds | 80 guests split into 6–10-person teams | organiser moves the game indoors / provides capes, per their own guidance | from ~3 290 PLN per attraction (catalogue) |
| Culinary / barmańskie workshops | per-person workshops | min. 10–15 per group | indoor by default | published per person (349–569 PLN/person) — too costly for 80 at this budget |
| Vistula cruise / PRL-themed party | per-attraction packages | 80 guests | weather-dependent | catalogue per-attraction prices far above the allocation |

Why the city game: it is the only format in the organiser's catalogue that scales to 80
people at the hard budget ceiling, keeps the whole group active at once, and comes with
the insurance/coordinator/Plan-B package that the requirements demand.
