Venue

Villa Foksal (Foksal 3/5, central Warsaw) — a historic villa with combinable restaurant rooms, a light-filled orangery and a garden, hosting up to 400 people. The venue's own kitchen handles food and diet arrangements, and for outdoor events the team always agrees a parallel under-roof variant — the indoor twin this day requires.

  • Garden for the outdoor blocks; orangery and rooms as the weather twin
  • In-house catering — menus, service times and diets with one team
  • Corporate-event experience (conferences, galas, integration meetings)
  • Fallback option if the date is taken: a Warsaw hotel venue with garden, restaurant and wege/gluten-free menu variants

Weather outlook & contingency

Late April in Warsaw is mild but changeable: the outlook for 23 April 2027 shows +11.9 °C / +4.5 °C; the same date in past years was dry and overcast at +16.8…+21.4 °C with wind up to 24 km/h.

Contingency: the morning workshop is always indoors. The afternoon city game runs outdoors unless it rains or the feels-like temperature is below +8 °C — in that case the organiser's indoor variant runs instead. Same timetable either way.

Catering

Lean service level, prepared by the venue's in-house kitchen: 8 240 PLN (~103 PLN per participant).

  • 08:45 welcome coffee break — coffee, tea, water, pastries (vegetarian pastries, gluten-free option labelled)
  • 12:30 working lunch — hot buffet with two hot dishes (one vegetarian as a main), grains, salads, bread, fruit and drinks

Vegetarian and gluten-free options are arranged directly with the kitchen and labelled at the buffet.

Team-building activity

Warsaw city game, 13:45–16:15 — 80 participants split into 6–10-person teams solving puzzles and tasks across the venue grounds and the city, with a dedicated coordinator and participant insurance included. The organiser has run corporate events in Warsaw since 2006 and provides a weather plan B.

No live music, per the confirmed requirements — the evening program is simply the wrap-up, awards and a group photo at 16:15.

Logistics

  • AV (morning workshop) — projector, 2 × 2 m screen, sound system, two microphones and cable ramps, rented at published day rates (1 015 PLN)
  • Transport — two coaches, HQ → venue 07:45–08:45 and venue → HQ 16:45–17:30 (1 820 PLN at the published Warsaw hourly rate)
  • Staffing — venue service team plus the activity coordinator; one internal event lead as single point of contact
  • Permits — none required on the company side; the activity organiser handles any public-space permissions and insurance

Risks & notes

  • Venue quote pending — the room-and-service figure is a budget allocation (the venue quotes on request); if it comes in higher, the fallback reduction plan keeps the day under the ceiling.
  • City-game quote pending — allocation at the organiser's lowest published per-attraction price point.
  • April weather — every outdoor block has an indoor twin and the game's decision rule does not change the timetable.
  • AV delivery fee — avoided by host-collected pickup.
